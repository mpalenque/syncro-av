Sync A/V

How we perceive sound and image as one

A talk by Manuel Palenque on why sound and image fuse, and how to use it in live performance.

Format
Talk with video examples
Duration
90 minutes
For
Musicians, creative coders, visual artists and designers

Seven principles

Mechanisms of multisensory integration. They act on every audience automatically, so they set the ground rules for any live audiovisual piece.

  1. Synchresis
  2. The ventriloquism effect
  3. The McGurk effect
  4. The temporal binding window
  5. Common fate
  6. Rhythmic entrainment
  7. Prediction and reward

Understanding why sound and image fuse turns every sync decision in a live set into a compositional one.

Manuel Palenque

Creative technologist and visual artist based in Buenos Aires. For over 15 years he has built real-time audiovisual performances, interactive installations and live shows, where music and image are generated together and respond to each other.
