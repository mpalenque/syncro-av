A talk by Manuel Palenque

Synchrony

Sound, image and perception in live performance.

Colapso — live A/V, real-time visuals

The talk

Sound and image reach us through separate senses, yet we perceive them as a single event. This talk looks at how that fusion happens, and how artists can work with it in live audiovisual performance.

Format
Talk with video examples
Duration
90 minutes
Audience
Musicians, VJs, visual artists and designers
Language
English or Spanish

Perception

Seven principles

Seven mechanisms of multisensory integration, each explained with video examples. They operate in every audience, automatically, which makes them the ground rules of any live audiovisual piece.

  1. Synchresis
  2. The ventriloquism effect
  3. The McGurk effect
  4. The temporal binding window
  5. Common fate
  6. Rhythmic entrainment
  7. Prediction and reward

The idea

Understanding why sound and image fuse turns every sync decision in a live set into a compositional one.
