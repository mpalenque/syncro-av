Sync A/V

How we perceive sound and image as one

A talk by Manuel Palenque on why sound and image fuse, and how to use it in live performance.

Format

Seven principles

Mechanisms of multisensory integration. They act on every audience automatically, so they set the ground rules for any live audiovisual piece.

  1. Synchresis
  2. The ventriloquism effect
  3. The McGurk effect
  4. The temporal binding window
  5. Common fate
  6. Rhythmic entrainment
  7. Prediction and reward

Understanding why sound and image fuse turns every sync decision in a live set into a compositional one.

Syllabus Breakdown

  1. Perceptual Mechanics of Fusion (45 min): Synchresis, the temporal binding window, rhythmic entrainment, McGurk & ventriloquism effects, and how prediction error drives audiovisual tension on stage.
  2. Real-Time Signal Architecture (60 min): Designing tightly coupled audio-visual-lighting loops using low-latency FFT/onset analysis, OSC, MIDI, and Art-Net/DMX state machines without pre-rendered timecode.
  3. Generative Visuals & 3D Stage Digital Twins (45 min): Real-time particle physics, custom shaders (vvvv / WebGPU / GLSL), and building a 3D Digital Twin of the physical venue to pre-visualize screens, lasers, and DMX fixtures prior to load-in.
  4. Live Stage Lab & Performance (30 min Lab + 45 min Show): Participants test their own laptops, synths, or controllers directly on the live audiovisual + DMX rig, culminating in the 45-minute COLAPSO Live A/V performance.

Technical Requirements (Classroom & Stage)

  • What Manuel Brings (Carry-On Fly-In Rig): Apple Silicon MacBook Pro (M4 Pro), compact MIDI/OSC controllers, low-latency USB-C audio interface + DMX/Art-Net node, and custom real-time A/V software engine.
  • Space & Projection: Auditorium, black box, or studio with controlled darkness; 1080p60/4K60 projection or LED screen via single HDMI or SDI connection at the presenter table.
  • Sound System: Full-range stereo PA with subwoofers (2× balanced XLR/DI inputs from artist interface) for accurate sub-bass and transient perception experiments.
  • Lighting (Optional / Performance): DMX512 or Art-Net input for house fixtures/strobes/lasers + haze (if hosting the 45-min Live A/V performance).
